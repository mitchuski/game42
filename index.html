THE NEXT CHAPTER · IN DEVELOPMENT

Your work has a place
in your Star.

We’re connecting the game to Star Key and Hearthold: a contribution you choose to sign, a place that receives it with consent, and a record you can keep.

  1. 01 / GAME

    Make a contribution.

    Agree a task, bring evidence and ask someone to review the result.

  2. 02 / STAR KEY

    Choose what you sign.

    Preview the record and approve its signature through your extension.

  3. 03 / HEARTHOLD

    Give it a destination.

    Hearthold checks permission before carrying out the agreed action. A receipt records what happened.

  4. 04 / STAR HOLD

    Keep the signed record.

    Your private Hold retains the contribution and receipt. Your Star shows what is held and what has been checked.

Today: practise, review and export locally. Extension signing, Hearthold submission and Hold saving are being connected; this game does not perform them yet.

A game an agent can host

We’re designing Territory for a human or agent facilitator to guide turns, suggest tasks and prepare a session recap. Participants choose their commitments; reviewers make their own decisions. Each signer speaks for their own part.

A signature identifies who signed a particular record. Acceptance of the work and permission to act remain separate decisions.
